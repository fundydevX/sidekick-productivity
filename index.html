Good to know

Frequently asked

Is my data private?

Yes. Tasks, notes and categories are stored locally on your device. AI features only send the text needed to answer your request, and you can back everything up or wipe it at any time from Settings.

Does Sidekick work offline?

Completely. Task management, notes, calendar, reminders and insights all work without a connection. Only the AI agent and live weather need the internet.

What's the difference between Plus and Pro?

Plus is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited categories, deeper nesting, the 3D emoji pack and full personalisation. Pro is a monthly subscription that adds the AI Agent, premium voices, image upload, weather and AI Insights.

Can I back up my tasks?

Yes — export a full backup file at any time, restore it on another device, or copy everything out as Markdown.

Which devices are supported?

Sidekick is available on Android through Google Play, with light and dark themes that adapt to your system.
